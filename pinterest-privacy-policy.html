Privacy Policy

Application: YY DESIGN Content Publisher
Operated by: YY DESIGN (YY Design Group (HK) Co. Ltd.)
Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the YY DESIGN Content Publisher application ("the App") handles information. The App is an internal publishing tool used solely by YY DESIGN to manage its own Pinterest business account through the Pinterest API.

1. Information We Access

Through the Pinterest API, and only with the account owner's authorization, the App may access:

  • Basic profile information of our own Pinterest business account (username, account type);
  • Our own boards and Pins, including content we create, publish, or schedule through the App;
  • Performance and analytics data relating to our own account, boards, and Pins.

The App does not access the accounts, boards, or Pins of any other Pinterest user.

2. How We Use Information

Information accessed through the App is used only to:

  • Create, publish, and schedule Pins on our own Pinterest business account;
  • Review the performance of our own published content;
  • Operate, maintain, and secure the App.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share any information obtained through the Pinterest API with third parties. Access credentials are used only by our own team and our authorized automation tools acting on our behalf.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Access tokens are stored securely and are used only for the purposes described above. Analytics data is retained only for as long as needed for internal reporting. We do not build profiles of Pinterest users, and we do not collect personal data from visitors of our Pins beyond the aggregate analytics provided by Pinterest.

5. Your Choices

The account owner may revoke the App's access at any time through Pinterest account settings (Apps / connected applications). Upon revocation, the App can no longer access the account.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this address, with the "Last updated" date revised accordingly.
