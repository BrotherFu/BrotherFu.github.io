Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Brother Fu Uploader is a private automation tool used solely by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, "Brother Fu's Theater".

Information We Access

Through the YouTube Data API, this application accesses only the owner's own YouTube account, solely to upload videos, set video metadata (titles, descriptions) and set custom thumbnails, as instructed by the owner.

How Information Is Used

Access is used exclusively to operate the owner's channel. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. No information about any other person is collected or stored.

Data Storage

Authorization tokens are stored securely by the owner's assistant platform and are used only to perform the uploads described above. The owner may revoke access at any time from their Google Account permissions page.
